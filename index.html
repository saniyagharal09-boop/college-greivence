ABOUT THE SYSTEM

A better way to handle college grievances

Our system provides a structured and transparent way for students to report campus issues and monitor their resolution.

🔒

Secure

Secure access with an optional anonymity mode for sensitive reports.

📋

Structured

Complaints are organized, tracked and routed to the appropriate department.

📊

Transparent

Students can follow the complaint lifecycle from submission to resolution.

KEY FEATURES

Everything in one centralized platform

Designed around the Student, Faculty and Admin views described in the project plan.

01

Complaint Submission

Simple structured form for reporting college issues.

02

Ticket Tracking

Each grievance receives a distinct tracking ticket.

03

Smart Routing

Keyword-based routing can direct complaints to departments.

04

Status Workflow

Follow Submitted, Under Review and Resolved stages.

05

Escalation

Delayed complaints can be escalated according to an SLA.

06

Admin Reports

Monitor open, closed and department performance metrics.
